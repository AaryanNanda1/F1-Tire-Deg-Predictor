# Feature-engineering report

This report is generated by `analysis/run_feature_analysis.py` from the committed Ground Effect store. The target is continuous `LapTimeDelta`; the analysis uses time-series-inspired feature construction and chronological event-level validation, not a strict sequence model.

## Scope and motivation

The goal is to understand redundancy among tire-age, race-progress, weather, circuit, compound, driver, and team inputs, then test whether a lower-dimensional circuit representation improves generalization. The central modeling difficulty is separating tire degradation from fuel load, circuit stress, weather, session context, and driver or constructor pace.

## Leakage controls

`StintLength`, `NormalizedTyreLife`, session-derived pace baselines, `EventName`, `EventDate`, and the target are excluded from predictive matrices. Circuit PCA is descriptive when fit on the complete circuit table, but predictive PCA is refit inside each training fold. Marginal correlations are descriptive and are not treated as feature importance.

## Results

All numeric values in this report must be read from the CSV files in `tables/` and `metrics/`, and all figures are generated from those files. PCA signs are arbitrary. The circuit sample is small (approximately 25 profiles), so component interpretations are directional rather than causal. The provisional production decision is to retain the raw representation unless the completed downstream cliff and strategy benchmarks show a consistent benefit for a candidate.

See `provenance.json` for the exact input hash, software versions, seed, and fold definition. The report runner intentionally leaves production models and processed stores unchanged.
